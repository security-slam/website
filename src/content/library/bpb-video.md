Dr. David Wheeler walks through the fundamentals of the OSPS Baseline and how to use the Best Practices Badge portal to self-attest to your project's OSPS Baseline status.

Self-attesting on the Best Practices Badge was recommended during the Spring 2026 Slam, but it does not earn the stricter Fall 2026 [Defender badge](/library/defender), which requires a passing scan published on grc.store. The Best Practices Badge is still a useful way to distribute your self-attestations on the controls the scanner cannot check.
