## Challenge

Implement the CRA guidelines provided by the project steward

## How?

<!-- TODO (REV-458): confirm with the project steward what "the CRA guidelines" are, then replace this with a numbered checklist. Depth lives in the CRA Readiness Guide linked below, not here. -->

1. Read the [CRA Readiness Guide](/library/cra-readiness-guide).
2. Work through the checklist in the guide and link each artefact from your `security-insights.yml`.
3. Submit your completion below.

**Prefer a head start?** The Slam's [AI skills](/library/ai-skills) include a `cra` skill that drafts a voluntary CRA readiness document, with the required disclaimer, from what it can verify in your repository. Treat its output as a first draft, not a finished document: the skill makes guesses about your project's practices, so check every claim before you publish it. It will leave out any practice it can't find evidence for, so if something is missing, that's your cue to add the evidence rather than the sentence.
